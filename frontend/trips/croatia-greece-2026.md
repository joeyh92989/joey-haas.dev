## Day 1 · Arrive · Dubrovnik

Landed late and walked the Stradun at midnight when it was finally empty. The
limestone is polished to glass by four centuries of feet; in the dark it looks
wet.

## Day 2 · The walls · Dubrovnik

Two kilometres around the top of the old town, started at eight to beat the
heat and the cruise ships. The north side looks down into back gardens and
laundry; the south side is nothing but sea.

![City walls, late afternoon](walls-afternoon 'Limestone polished to glass, people on the wall above')
![Lokrum from Minčeta](lokrum-from-minceta)

## Day 4 · Travel day · Dubrovnik → Athens → Naxos

Flight to Athens, taxi to Piraeus, the Blue Star out of gate E7 at half five.
Five and a half hours; the back deck is the whole point. Into Naxos harbour
after dark with the Portara lit up on its islet.

## Day 5 · Chora · Naxos

Did nothing on purpose. Coffee by the harbour, the Kastro's alleys until we
were lost, the Portara at sunset with everyone else. The marble doorway is all
that was built of a temple to Apollo in the sixth century BC.

![Portara at sunset](portara 'All that was built of a temple to Apollo')

## Day 8–9 · Slow days · Naxos

Repeat of the good parts. One more swim, one more sunset at the Portara, a
last bag of cheese pies for the ferry.

## Day 12 · Home · Athens

Breakfast in Plaka, the flea market at Monastiraki, airport by two.
